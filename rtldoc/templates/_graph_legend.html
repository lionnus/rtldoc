{# The legend of the block diagram. The module page and the overview share it. #}

The ports stand at the walls of the module frame: input, left · output, right · interface — a slanted green box is a stream or a bus; the wide green line carries its data, and the arrow gives the direction · control — the dashed amber line shows which module controls what · flag / status · no direction. A straight box is a module. A hexagon is a port that goes in two directions. A dashed inner box is a generate block. The dashed white node is the logic of the module itself: the processes that drive or read a control net. The clock, reset, clear and test-mode nets are not in the graph; the chips below the diagram give them.
